3D Games 🎮🌐

Immersive Real-World Simulation in Games

1. What are 3D Games?

3D games are games that simulate real-world depth using X, Y, and Z axes, allowing realistic movement and camera perspectives.

2. 3D Coordinate System

X-axis → left / right
Y-axis → up / down
Z-axis → forward / backward (depth)
