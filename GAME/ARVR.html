AR / VR 🥽🌐

Immersive Future of Gaming & Tech

1. What is AR & VR?

AR (Augmented Reality) overlays digital objects on the real world. VR (Virtual Reality) creates a fully virtual world.

2. AR vs VR

AR 🟢 → Real world + digital objects
VR 🔵 → Fully digital world

AR example:
- Pokémon GO 📱

VR example:
- Meta Quest games 🥽

3. Devices Used

VR Headsets 🥽 → Meta Quest, HTC Vive
AR Devices 📱 → Mobile cameras, AR glasses
Controllers 🎮 → hand tracking, motion input

4. Core AR/VR Concepts

Tracking 📍 → head + hand movement
Rendering 🎨 → real-time 3D graphics
Latency ⏱️ → delay must be very low
Immersion 🌐 → feeling “inside” the game

5. VR Game Systems

6. AR Systems
