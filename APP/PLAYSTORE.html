Google Play Store Guide 📱

Publish Android Apps Worldwide

1. What is Play Store?

Google Play Store is the official platform to publish and distribute Android apps.

2. Requirements

- Google Developer Account ($25 one-time)
- Signed APK or AAB file
- App privacy policy

3. Steps to Publish

1. Build app (Android Studio / Flutter)
2. Generate AAB file
3. Create Play Console account
4. Upload app bundle
5. Fill details (name, description)
6. Submit for review

4. App Review

- Google checks security
- Checks policy compliance
- Approval takes few hours to days

5. After Publishing

- App becomes live globally
- Users can download it
- You can update anytime
